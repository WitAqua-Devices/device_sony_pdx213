# Fingerprint
# The Egistec HIDL wrapper this depends on is deliberately not listed: the
# stock one was built against the Android 11 libhidlbase and cannot register
# its service.  Leaving it out lets soong resolve the dependency to the
# hidl_interface from hardware/sony/interfaces, the way pdx225 does.
